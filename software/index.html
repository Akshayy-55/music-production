Software comparison

Pick one DJ app and one DAW for 30 days. Concepts transfer — tool hopping does not.

  • Pick ONE DJ app and ONE DAW for 30 days. Switching weekly kills progress.
  • Mixxx + LMMS (or Cakewalk on Windows) is a complete free path.
  • Paid tools are optional upgrades after you finish a mix or track.
