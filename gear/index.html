Gear guide (India)

Prices are approximate India street/online ranges (2025–2026) and change often. Skills before spend — finish Shared fundamentals with laptop + headphones first.

  • Skills before spend
  • One tool at a time — don't buy a studio you won't use
  • Protect hearing: moderate volume, breaks every 60 minutes
  • Prefer official stores and known marketplaces; avoid cracked software 'deals'

1.₹0 — Start today

₹0
  • Laptop or desktop you already own
  • Any headphones or earbuds you have (volume carefully)
  • Mixxx (free DJ software)
  • One free DAW: LMMS, Cakewalk (Windows), or Waveform Free

You can reach first mix and first track on this tier.

2.~₹2–5k — Better headphones

₹2,000–5,000
  • Closed-back practice headphones (Audio-Technica / Sony / similar entry models)
  • Optional: simple Y-splitter if sharing with a phone

Closed-backs help cue mixing and night practice without leaking as much.

3.~₹5–15k — First hardware step

₹5,000–15,000
  • Used/entry DJ controller (Numark-class / similar) OR
  • USB audio interface for cleaner monitoring and mic/line in
  • Still optional — software practice comes first

Buy only after you enjoy daily practice for a few weeks.

4.~₹25–40k — When you are ready

₹25,000–40,000
  • Pioneer DDJ-FLX4-class or similar 2-channel controller
  • Or a stronger interface + monitors later
  • Budget for cables, stands, and hearing protection

Worth it when you know you will keep DJing or producing — not Day 1.
